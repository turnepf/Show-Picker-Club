Show Picker Club — SMS Messaging

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Show Picker Club is a TV-show and movie tracker. We use SMS for one thing: sending login verification codes.

How you opt in

SMS is optional. You may add a mobile number to your account to receive login codes by text. Members who don't provide a number sign in with an emailed code, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google instead, so using the service does not require consenting to SMS. The consent language presented when adding a number reads:

"I agree to receive text messages from Show Picker Club at the number provided, consisting of login verification codes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms."

Consent to receive these messages is not a condition of using Show Picker Club, and there is nothing for sale on the service.

What we send

  • Login codes: a one-time verification code when you sign in.

Message frequency varies and depends on how often you sign in by text — typically a handful of messages per month at most. We send nothing else: no alerts, no marketing, no reminders.

Opting out & help

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You'll get one confirmation and then no further messages.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email patrick@patrickturner.net.

Costs

Show Picker Club does not charge for messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing. Phone numbers are used only to deliver login codes. See the full Privacy Policy.
