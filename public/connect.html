Connection guide

Show Picker Club runs the connection for you. There's nothing to install from us.

  1. Add the connector

    Tap the button, then Add. It opens Claude with the name and address already filled in.

    Add to Claude

  2. Or enter it yourself

    In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, name it Show Picker Club and paste this address:

    https://showpicker.club/mcp

  3. Sign in and choose access

    Claude sends you to Show Picker Club. Sign in the way you usually do, then choose read-only or read-and-change.

  4. Try it

    Turn the connector on in a chat and ask: “What's on my Show Picker Next Up list?”

Good questions, clear answers

How AI apps work with Show Picker Club

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is how AI apps connect to other services. It lets your AI app use Show Picker Club's tools — read a list, add a show, rate it — on your behalf.

Does the AI live inside Show Picker?

No. You chat in your own AI app. Show Picker Club keeps the lists, so every change shows up in the app on every device.

What can it change?

Only your own shows and groups, and only if you allow changes when you connect. Choose read-only and it can look but not touch.

Can it see other people's notes?

No. It sees your own private notes, because it's acting as you. Other people's lists it sees only through groups you share, and never their notes.

What do I need?

A free Show Picker Club account and an AI app that supports custom MCP connectors. Some apps need a paid plan for that.

How do I disconnect?

On Connected apps, or in the iPhone app's account menu under AI MCP…. It stops working right away. Unused connections expire after 90 days.

Why do I need Developer mode?

That's where ChatGPT keeps custom connectors for apps that aren't in its directory. Whether you see it depends on your plan and, for work accounts, your administrator.

Does this set up Codex cloud?

No. These steps configure Codex on your own computer. A cloud task runs in its own environment and doesn't inherit your local servers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Add the connector once on claude.ai in a browser, and the Claude app on your phone and Mac picks it up.

Under the hood

Everything it can do

The connection acts as you, with the access you have in the app.

Read

  • get_profile — who the connection acts as, list counts, groups
  • list_my_shows — your lists, with your notes (paged)
  • get_show — one show: overview, cast, where it streams, ratings
  • list_member_shows — a group-mate's lists, without their notes
  • search_libraries — search your and your group-mates' lists
  • search_titles — search the TV and movie catalog
  • get_trending — what the club or one of your groups is adding
  • list_groups, get_group, get_group_recommendations — your groups and their Watch Next boards

Change (only if you allow it)

  • add_show, update_show, move_show, reorder_list, rate_show — your own shows
  • archive_show, restore_show, delete_show — take shows off your lists or bring them back
  • recommend_to_group, respond_to_recommendation, remove_recommendation — group Watch Next boards
  • create_group, create_group_invite, leave_group — your groups

It can't join a group for you, rename or delete a group, change your household or touch your account. Connected apps get up to 1,000 changes and 1,000 catalog searches a day, resetting at midnight UTC; reading your lists doesn't count toward either. What's shared and how it's handled: Privacy Policy. Your lists, your rules

Understand what you share

Connecting an AI app gives it access to your lists through the tools you allow. Choose an app you trust.

When you connect an AI app, it can read your lists — including your private notes — and, if you allow it, change them. What it reads through the connection is processed by that app's maker under their own policies. Review the app's approval settings: some apps make changes without asking you first. You can make the connection read-only when you sign in, and disconnect it any time from Connected apps.
