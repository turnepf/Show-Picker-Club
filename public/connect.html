Use Show Picker Club from your AI app

Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code to your lists. Then just ask — “what's on my Next Up that's under an hour?” — or tell it to add a show, and it happens in the app.

Claude

Tap the button, then Add. Claude sends you here to sign in and choose what it's allowed to do.

Add to Claude

Needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Button not working? In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, name it Show Picker Club and paste this URL:

https://showpicker.club/mcp

On iPhone, add it on claude.ai in a browser once — it then works in the Claude app too.

If the Show Picker Club app opens instead of a sign-in page, your device is holding an old copy of the app’s link list. Add the connector from claude.ai in Chrome instead, which always opens the sign-in page in the browser. Updating the app fixes it for good.

ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT's settings, find Developer mode (under Apps or Connectors, then Advanced) and turn it on.
  2. Choose Create, name it Show Picker Club, paste the URL below and pick OAuth.
  3. Sign in here when ChatGPT asks.
https://showpicker.club/mcp

Needs ChatGPT Plus or higher.

Claude Code

Run this once in a terminal, then use /mcp in Claude Code to sign in:

claude mcp add --transport http showpicker https://showpicker.club/mcp

Things to try

  • “What's on my Next Up list? Pick me something funny.”
  • “Add The Bear to my Watching list.”
  • “I finished Severance — move it to Loved and rate it a 9.”
  • “What are people in my groups watching that I'm not?”
  • “Recommend Slow Horses to my book club group.”

What it can do

The connection acts as you, with the access you have in the app. When you connect, you choose read-only or read-and-change.

Read

  • get_profile — who the connection acts as, list counts, groups
  • list_my_shows — your lists, with your notes (paged)
  • get_show — one show: overview, cast, where it streams, ratings
  • list_member_shows — a group-mate's lists, without their notes
  • search_libraries — search your and your group-mates' lists
  • search_titles — search the TV and movie catalog
  • get_trending — what the club or one of your groups is adding
  • list_groups, get_group, get_group_recommendations — your groups and their Watch Next boards

Change (only if you allow it)

  • add_show, update_show, move_show, reorder_list, rate_show — your own shows
  • archive_show, restore_show, delete_show — take shows off your lists or bring them back
  • recommend_to_group, respond_to_recommendation, remove_recommendation — group Watch Next boards
  • create_group, create_group_invite, leave_group — your groups

It can't join a group for you, rename or delete a group, change your household or touch your account. Connected apps get up to 500 actions, 100 changes and 100 catalog searches a day, resetting at midnight UTC.
